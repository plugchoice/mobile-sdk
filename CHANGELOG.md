## 0.3.0 (2026-10-07)

The first public version of the Plugchoice Mobile SDK, in beta.

- iOS (`PlugchoiceSDK`), Android (`com.plugchoice:plugchoice`) and React Native (`@plugchoice/react-native`).
- Link, Plugchoice's guided charger setup: add a charger, set it up, connect it to Wi-Fi, reconnect it.
- Client sessions fetched through one callback, scoped by your backend.
- Native Wi-Fi hotspot joins, local network discovery and sessions, Bluetooth LE, raw TCP and UDP, QR scanning.
- The bridge protocol, version 1 (`bridge/PROTOCOL.md`).
